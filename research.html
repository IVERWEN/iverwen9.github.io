A research on improving the precision of laser tracker in alignment based on Pareto improvement principle ↗

Review of Scientific Instruments · 95 (10), 105112

Wen Zhong, Wenjie Cheng, Tianyu Gao, Xin Zhang, Jianjun Mao, Zhijun Qi, Zengquan Liu, Hang Yu, Ming Liu & Wei Wang

DOI: 10.1063/5.0207241 ↗

In brief

This study develops a Pareto-based optimisation method for laser-tracker placement. It combines measurement-error modelling, a modified NSGA-II search and experimental validation to improve alignment and control-network measurements.

50%

Lower control-point uncertainty

Up to 50% in the positioning conditions investigated; results depend on measurement geometry.
